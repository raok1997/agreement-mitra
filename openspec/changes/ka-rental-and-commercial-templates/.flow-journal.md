# Flow journal — ka-rental-and-commercial-templates

## propose — 2026-09-18T08:23:02.008Z  [✅]
Outcome: Drafted the full change for adding Karnataka: proposal, five spec deltas, design and tasks. Karnataka gets residential and commercial agreement templates plus the stamp duty rules that price them, with the exact legal duty pre-selected at checkout and lower values available as an acknowledged override.
Decisions: Kept as ONE CR (touches no aggregate, endpoint or FSM transition; neither half ships alone). Per-slab min/max added to the rule schema rather than a Karnataka DutyExtension or splitting the rule in two -- the INR 500 cap in Art 30(1)(i) binds only the residential first slab, and the rule-level maximum would wrongly cap the 13-60 month slab. Karnataka uses the default PLANNED offer over an ANY_AMOUNT e-stamp medium plus a DENOMINATIONS paper medium (user decision 2026-09-18: exact amount by default, customer may override); the denominations medium is load-bearing because StampOptions builds the below-duty overrides from DENOMINATIONS media only. Both Karnataka statutory sections are mandatory, because the state_type layers remove the national stampRegistrationClause and an opt-in section would leave a Karnataka deed with no stamp clause at all -- the exact defect Telangana recorded. Registration threshold is 12 months (unamended Registration Act s.17(1)(d)), not Telangana's 0. Counterpart duty modelled flat at INR 500 as a deliberate over-reading, since DutyBasis.counterparts has no call site above 1. Every figure ships counselReview: null.
Halts: One halt, before proposing: asked the user how Karnataka's stamp should be offered and fulfilled. Answer: exact amount by default with a customer override, which maps onto the existing PLANNED offer mode. Scope-split halt was considered and deliberately not taken -- the CR scope heuristics are not tripped and the reasoning is recorded in proposal.md.
