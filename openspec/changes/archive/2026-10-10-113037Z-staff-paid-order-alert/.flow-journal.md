Outcome: Built the staff alert: a 30-second job finds payment orders the gateway marked paid and posts one message per agreement to the staff Discord channel, with retries and a hard stop. Each Spring context has its own database (HarnessTestConfig), so the cross-test interference the tasks guard against is confined to the one test class. The new FK was classified 'excluded' in AgreementForeignKeyGuardIntegrationTest, as design D9 already reasoned. The PAYMENT_MODE template fix the review journal called 'in the working tree' is already committed (38986ed).
